# 🐾 Cat Personality Recommender

## **Find your perfect feline friend!**  
Welcome to PlentyOfCats! 🐱😸😺😻😼😽

This project helps you find the best cat match based on your current cat’s personality. Whether you're adopting a second cat or just curious, my model analyzes personality traits and recommends cats that are **most similar** or **totally opposite**—your choice! 🐱💕



## ✨ What This Project Does
- Uses data from **2,800+ cats** with **52 rated personality traits**
- Groups traits into **4 easy-to-understand personality factors**
- Lets you **rate your cat**, then finds cats with:
  - ✅ **Similar personalities** *(default)*  
  - 🔄 **Opposite personalities** *(optional)*
- Customizable filters for:
  - 🎯 Focusing on specific traits
  - 🐱 Matching by cat sex
  - 🔢 Choosing how many matches to see



## 🧠 How It Works
I used **PCA + Factor Analysis** to reduce 52 traits into **4 main personality factors**:

1. 😿 **Anxiety & Fearfulness** – How cautious or stressed a cat tends to be towards humans   
2. 😾**Dominance & Aggression** – Interaction style with other cats  
3. 😸 **Activity Level** – Couch potato or zoomies all the time?  
4. 😻 **Social Reactivity** – How cats respond to other pets  

These factors make recommendations **more accurate** and **easier to interpret**.
